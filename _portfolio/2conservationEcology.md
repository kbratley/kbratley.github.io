I also work on conservation projects that help natural resource managers make better decisions with their data. At Saguaro National Park, I used decades of Landsat satellite data and statistical modeling to figure out which invasive species treatments actually work best. The analysis showed park managers where to focus their efforts and budget to get the biggest impact for native plant recovery. This kind of work demonstrates how satellite data and statistics can turn complex ecological questions into practical management guidance.
